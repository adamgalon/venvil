    Proposals: more out of each preset built and checked in the tibia.com planner, Nov 24

    The rules that decide it (from the planner's own help texts): a Revelation perk reaches stage 1 at 250 points in its domain, stage 2 at 500 and stage 3 at 1,000, and a gem's Revelation Mastery counts toward that. Your two Revelation Mastery gems are +150 each, so 850 points in a domain plus the gem make stage 3. A slice's conviction perk only works when the slice is full. Your damage comes from distance (165 in the main set before Sharpshooter) far more than from magic level (39 + 4 holy), so the Ethereal and Strong Ethereal Spear augments are worth more to you than the Divine Barrage one, and mitigation multiplier is close to worthless: your base mitigation is 1.3%, so +27.8% of it is about 0.4% less damage.

    1. Hunting: Divine Empowerment (the proposed "exp"). It's the only build with two Revelations at once, because gem 5 pays 150 of the 1,000 points: Divine Empowerment 3 plus Gift of Life 1. It also has the most damage and healing (+27) and augments on the spells you actually cast.

    2. Bosses: Avatar of Light (the proposed "boss"). Gem 7 pays 150 points here too, and the build carries Strong Ethereal Spear II and Ballistic Mastery, both made for single targets.

    3. Third preset: Gift of Life, in the unused "Default-Preset" slot. Use it on new or dangerous spawns: a killing blow heals you 30% once per 10 h of fighting, instead of 20% once per 30 h. You give up the +12% damage field for Positional Tactics (distance +3) and 100 more HP. Its code keeps gem 3 in the top-left vessel, where resonance III turns on its Salvation +6%.

    4. Divine Grenade: skip it. It costs all 1,000 points because none of your gems helps, it's holy damage that grows with magic level (39) rather than distance (165), it uses the same 2 s attack cooldown as your barrages, the top-right domain has no greater gem, and the build has no Gift of Life at all. Its only bright spot, Ethereal Barrage II, isn't worth that.
